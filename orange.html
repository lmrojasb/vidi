Research contributions

Orange leads overall project coordination and brings dialogue-system expertise to VIDI. The team contributes to collecting and annotating user-centered data, developing situated dialogue, integrating the planned proof of concept, and evaluating the interaction with users.

The proposal describes a prototype pipeline connecting wearable-device input, speech recognition, navigation and dialogue models, and spoken output.
