Research contributions

IRIT leads the project’s data collection and human evaluation work packages. The proposal plans to co-design indoor assistance scenarios, collect and annotate dialogues, and evaluate the utility and usability of the proof of concept with visually impaired participants.

The proposed studies and recruitment depend on ethics review, data-protection approval and participant recruitment.
